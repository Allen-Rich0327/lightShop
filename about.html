About Gloris Lighting

Decorative lighting manufacturer in Zhongshan, China, with 20+ years of OEM/ODM experience.

Our Story

Built on manufacturing, not assembly

Founded in 2005, Gloris Lighting operates a vertically integrated facility in Guzhen, China's lighting capital. From die-casting and CNC machining to surface treatment, wiring and assembly — every step happens under one roof.

We work with lighting brands, distributors and project buyers across the UK, Canada, US and Europe. Our in-house R&D team of 15 engineers turns design concepts into shippable SKUs with 7–10 day prototype turnaround.

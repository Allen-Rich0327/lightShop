FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

Straight answers on MOQ, OEM, certifications and lead times.

Can you provide OEM and ODM lighting services?

Yes. We support OEM/ODM from concept, drawings or reference samples through tooling, certification and mass production — all in-house in Guzhen.

What MOQ do you support?

MOQ depends on category and customization. Catalogue items allow flexible trial quantities; OEM programmes typically start from negotiated project volumes.

Which certifications are available?

We prepare CE, RoHS, UL, SAA and ETL documentation by destination market, with test reports maintained per SKU where required.

How fast can I get a sample?

Standard sampling runs 7–10 days for most finishes. Complex tooling or new molds may take longer — we confirm timelines at consultation.
